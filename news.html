⭐ Our Story

From demolition to celebration — and back again

📅 Summer 2024  ·  🕐 5 min read  ·  Community

In August 2020, residents formed Friends of Badger Park with a simple goal: replace the playground that Chesterfield Borough Council had to remove on safety grounds. What followed was an extraordinary four years of community spirit — raffles, grant applications, 5,000 daffodil bulbs planted, a successful funding bid, and a brand-new playground opened in February 2022.

But the story isn't without its setbacks. In May 2023 the community orchard was vandalised. Then in October 2023 an arson attack destroyed the slide and caused significant damage. The group was shaken — but never stopped. As we publish our very first newsletter in Summer 2024, we are stronger than ever.
