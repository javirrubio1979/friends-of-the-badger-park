About Us

Who we are, what we believe, and why we love Badger Park.

Our mission

Friends of Badger Park exists to protect, enhance and celebrate Badger Park as a vital green space for the people of Chesterfield. We believe that a well-loved park makes for a stronger, healthier, happier community.

We were formed in 2020 in response to the removal of the park's playground — and we've never stopped. From fundraising and grant-writing to planting days and community events, everything we do is powered by volunteers who care deeply about this place.

  • 🌿 Care for the natural environment and local wildlife
  • 🤝 Build an inclusive, welcoming community space
  • 🎉 Create events that bring neighbours together
  • 📣 Campaign for ongoing investment in the park

Our story

Aug 2020

Founded by local residents

After Chesterfield Borough Council announced the playground's removal, a group of neighbours came together to replace it. Goal: raise £3,000 to unlock up to £30,000 in further funding.

Oct–Dec 2020

First fundraising push

5,000 daffodil bulbs planted with CBC. A Christmas raffle raised £1,441. A £5,000 CBC International Levy Fund award brought the total to over £7,000.

2021

Design competition & funding success

Local primary schools entered a playground design competition. In July, the funding bid was successful! Halloween Xplorer, Giant Land Art, bat & bird box sessions all ran this year.

Feb 2022

New playground officially opens 🎉

After over a year of construction and community effort, the brand-new playground opened on a cold but fantastic day of celebration. The community had done it.

2022–2023

Going from strength to strength

Easter Egg Hunts, a Jubilee Picnic, tree planting festival, sports days, and the hugely popular Colour Run in August 2023. The park was thriving.

Oct 2023

Setback: arson attack on playground

Devastatingly, an arson attack blew out the slide and caused significant damage to the playground. The group was shaken — but not stopped.

2024 →

Stronger than ever

Undeterred, the group continues to plan events, fundraise, and create a fabulous community space for everyone in Chesterfield.
